# Problem Statement

Businesses work with multiple partners to manage orders, supplies and deliveries, but when each partner uses different systems and processes, sharing information and resolving issues together becomes difficult.

## Who is affected?

- Manufacturers
- Component and material suppliers
- Procurement teams
- Production and operations teams
- Logistics and delivery partners

## Illustrative example

A buyer requires 1,000 units by a specified deadline. A supplier can provide 700 units by that date, leaving 300 units unfulfilled.

The organisations need to review the order, availability, delivery commitments and business constraints before agreeing on a response.

Possible responses may include partial delivery with a backorder, expedited supply, an approved alternative supplier, or production rescheduling.

## Important context

Businesses already use ERP systems, EDI, APIs, integration platforms, supplier portals and partner networks to exchange information and coordinate work. BridgeSync is proposed as a focused shared case workflow for investigating exceptions, coordinating partner decisions and tracking approved follow-through.

The quantities in this example are synthetic and do not describe a real company incident.
